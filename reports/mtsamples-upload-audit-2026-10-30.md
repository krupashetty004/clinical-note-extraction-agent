These are heuristic matches, not a PHI determination. Date matches may include ordinary clinical dates and false positives; phone-like matches also require manual local review. Zero matches do not prove that the dataset contains no personal information. Do not send these notes to an external AI provider until the data handling and provider use are approved.

## Label suitability and limitations

- The source specialty field is a source-provided category, not a clinical entity annotation.
- The keywords field is not validated entity ground truth.
- The CSV does not supply note-specific diagnosis, medication, procedure, allergy or vital-sign evidence spans, assertion/status labels, or independently validated ICD-10-CM labels.
- The generated 100 records are an **unannotated annotation queue**, not 100 gold-labelled clinical notes.
- The queue's specialty distribution meets the minimum coverage count, but it does not satisfy the clinical gold-standard labelling and adjudication requirements by itself.
- The audit does not measure extraction quality, model accuracy, clinical safety, or readiness for deployment.
